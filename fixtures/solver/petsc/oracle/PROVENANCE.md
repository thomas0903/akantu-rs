3.22.5, OpenMPI 5.0.7, one MPI process, reference commit

`host.eigen_cache_sizes`), `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=Haswell` and

(`scripts/mumps/blas-pin.sh`; OpenBLAS reports core `Haswell`, 1 thread:

identical; `static_default` differs in 11 of 100 displacements and the NR
`error`, `dynamic_default` in most values (energies 1,985 of 2,406) — the
OpenBLAS-core dependence that an independent review measured.
